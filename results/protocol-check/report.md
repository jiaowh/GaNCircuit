The scenario loop counts are inherited planning assumptions, **not estimated branch probabilities**. No 50-chip certificate path exists. Every nominal-passing certification and confirmation pays for 250 chips.

A job-runtime multiplier of 2 and two complete-workload retry copies produce a sixfold CPU stress case; this is sensitivity analysis, not a confidence bound or forecast. Timeouts supply a different, much larger operational ceiling. Recompute from measured per-stage attempts, failure rates and runtime distributions before launch. Acquisition/intervention/ablation rows are explicit spending caps, not unbounded extra sampling. GPU fits are enumerated separately; their duration is an unmeasured placeholder. Tool installation, implementation, covariance estimation, model inference, proposer API charges and BO refits are not priced as simulator time and require separate measured operating allocations before full launch.
